# On the day: a note for trainers

Winning with AI · Course check and Trainer check

## Before you start

- Ask the lead trainer for your group's links. They come from the Sheet: **Course check > Show the links**.
- Open the **projector link** on the room's screen. It shows a big QR code and the short link.
- Every link works on any phone, on any network.

## Which link, when

| When | Show this | Who |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day 1, before the opening talk | Projector: **start of the course** | Participants |
| Day 2, after the Module 4 close | Projector: **end of the course** | Participants |
| ToT Day 1, first thing | Projector: **trainer check start** | Trainers |
| ToT Day 2, at the end | Projector: **trainer check end** | Trainers |
| ToT Day 2, teach-backs | **Teach-back scoring** link, sent only to scorers | Lead trainer and peers |

Say this before each check:

- "There is no pass or fail. Answer alone, without looking anything up."
- "Use the **same email** at the start and the end. That is how we match your two results."
- It takes about 10 minutes (trainers: about 8).

## The scorer code

- The lead trainer sets it in the Sheet: **Course check > Set scorer code**.
- Tell it only to the people who score teach-backs. Never put it on the projector.
- Five wrong tries bring a 30-second wait. Then try again.
- After the training, the lead trainer sets a new code, so the old one stops working.

## Scoring a teach-back

1. Open the scoring link and type the code.
2. Write your name and choose **Lead trainer** or **Peer**. Choose the course part.
3. Choose the trainer from the list. Not there? Press **Refresh the list**. Still not there? Press **Someone is missing? Type their name**.
4. Choose 1, 2, 3 or 4 for each of the four lines. Add a comment if you like.
5. Press **Save score**. The form keeps your name and the part, and clears the rest for the next trainer.

## If the Wi-Fi fails

- Ask people to **turn off Wi-Fi and use their phone data**. The pages are small.
- Answers are kept on the phone while people answer. A refresh does not lose them.
- If saving fails, the page says "Your answers were not saved yet". Press **Try again** when the connection is back. It never saves twice.
- No phone? Pair people up: each person uses the phone in turn, with their own name and email.

## If something looks wrong

- "This check is closed": the cohort is closed in the Config tab. Ask the lead trainer to open it.
- "This page is out of date": reload the page.
- Anything else: note the time and the person's email, and tell the lead trainer.
